A demo concept for a family-run yard, with horses suited to riders just starting out and confident riders alike.

Beginners

Forest Amble

An easy, unhurried ride through the forest — a gentle introduction for first-time riders or anyone after a calm outing.

Confident riders

The Game Ride

A livelier ride up onto the escarpment with trotting and cantering, and a chance to spot game along the way.

Brackenridge Falls Ride

A longer half-day ride out to Brackenridge Falls, with a stop along the way to rest and enjoy a snack before heading back.
