How long does a carpet take to dry?

Usually four to six hours with windows open. We use a low-moisture method and bring air movers for larger rooms.

Do I need to be home?

For a first visit, yes — we walk the job with you. After that, many clients leave a key arrangement. Your call.

What about pets and children?

Our anti-bacterial products are chosen to be safe once dry. We tell you how long to keep the room clear.
