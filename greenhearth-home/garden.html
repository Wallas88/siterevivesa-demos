Before you book

Good to know.

Weekly or fortnightly?

Most gardens do well fortnightly in winter and weekly in summer. We suggest a rhythm after the first visit.

Do you take the green waste away?

Yes — clippings and cuttings leave with us, unless you compost and would rather keep them.

Can you do a once-off tidy-up?

Yes. A single big clean-up is a common first job; regular maintenance is optional after that.
