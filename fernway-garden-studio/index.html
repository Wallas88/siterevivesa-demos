Field Notes

On texture, and why it matters

Most gardens are judged on colour alone. This studio designs for texture first — the visual weight of a leaf from across the lawn, and the tactile quality of a bed once you're standing in it. Get the texture right and the colour takes care of itself.

It's a habit that comes from treating every garden like a drawing with layers — structure, then rhythm, then detail — rather than a shopping list of plants.
